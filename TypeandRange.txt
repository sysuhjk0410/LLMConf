Here is the meaning of max-num-batched-tokens in the vLLM Engine Arguments:
[--max-num-batched-tokens MAX_NUM_BATCHED_TOKENS]: Maximum number of batched tokens per iteration.

Here are some of the latest related research:
In the paper titled ‘Towards SLO-Optimized LLM Serving via Automatic Inference Engine Tuning,' published in 2024, it is pointed out that the range for max-num-batched-tokens (Integer) should be limited to [64, 8192].

Here are issues raised by users in the open-source community for LLMs:

Here is the pre-knowledge about the parameter max-num-batched-tokens:
The value of max-num-batched-tokens must be greater than or equal to the values of max-model-len and max-num-seqs.
